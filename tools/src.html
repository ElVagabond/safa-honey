01 — The Pantry

Every jar has a landscape inside.

Each honey tastes of the flowers the bees visited — dark and woody from the forest, light and floral from acacia, rich and buttery from sidr.

02 — Hive to Jar

Nothing added. Nothing taken away.

We keep the process short on purpose. The less we do to honey, the more of it reaches you.

03 — Our Story

The very best, from across India.

Safa began with a simple idea: to collect the finest floral honeys from around the country and bring them to people exactly as nature made them.

From our home on Kanakapura Road in Bangalore, we jar raw honey for families — and supply it to makers in food, healthcare and beauty care.
